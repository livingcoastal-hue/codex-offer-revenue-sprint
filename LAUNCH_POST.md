# LinkedIn Launch Post

I built a free Codex skill + agent bundle for founders, marketers, agencies, coaches, and consultants who want sharper offers and better conversion.

It is called **Offer Revenue Sprint**.

Drop in a landing page, sales page, email, ad, or rough offer, and Codex acts like an 8-agent growth team:

- ICP analyst
- Messaging strategist
- Conversion auditor
- Trust and proof builder
- Offer packaging strategist
- Email writer
- Ad creative strategist
- Sales objection handler

It can produce:

- an offer scorecard
- a landing page teardown
- headline and hook options
- an ad angle matrix
- a 7-email follow-up sequence
- a 7-day revenue sprint plan

The bundle includes:

- the Codex skill
- an orchestrator agent
- 8 specialist agent briefs
- reusable templates
- a launch-ready social card

The goal is simple:

Stop getting vague advice like "clarify your value prop."

Get specific fixes:

- what headline to use
- what proof is missing
- what CTA is too weak
- what offer stack to test
- what objections to handle
- what to fix first this week
