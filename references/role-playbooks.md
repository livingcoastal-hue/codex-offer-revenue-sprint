# Role Playbooks

## Scout, ICP Analyst

Use for audience clarity and buying context.

Outputs:
- Primary ICP and secondary ICP.
- Jobs-to-be-done, pains, desired outcomes, buying triggers.
- Awareness level: unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, most-aware.
- Objections and trust gaps.

## Hook, Messaging Strategist

Use for positioning and front-of-page copy.

Outputs:
- One-line positioning statement.
- Hero headline options.
- Subheadline options.
- Mechanism or unique angle.
- Promise and anti-promise: what the offer does and does not claim.

## Funnel, Conversion Auditor

Use for page and funnel diagnostics.

Outputs:
- Above-the-fold audit.
- CTA audit.
- Friction points.
- Missing page sections.
- Suggested page order.

## Proof, Trust Builder

Use for credibility and risk reversal.

Outputs:
- Proof inventory.
- Case study angles.
- Testimonial prompts.
- Guarantee or risk-reversal options.
- Authority and trust assets to add.

## Offer, Packaging Strategist

Use for pricing, packages, bonuses, and offer architecture.

Outputs:
- Core offer stack.
- Pricing tiers or package ladder.
- Bonus and urgency ideas.
- Guarantee and qualification rules.
- Good/better/best version.

## Email, Lifecycle Writer

Use for nurture, launch, reactivation, and follow-up.

Outputs:
- Subject lines.
- 3-7 email sequence.
- CTA for each email.
- Segmentation and timing notes.

## Ads, Creative Strategist

Use for paid and organic creative.

Outputs:
- Angle matrix by pain, desire, proof, objection, and contrarian belief.
- Short-form posts.
- Ad hooks.
- Creative brief.
- Test plan.

## Closer, Sales Enablement Lead

Use for conversion at the decision point.

Outputs:
- Objection handler.
- Discovery questions.
- DM or call follow-up scripts.
- CTA and close copy.
- Decision criteria checklist.
