# Copy Patterns

## Headline Patterns

- Get [specific outcome] without [common pain].
- For [ICP] who want [desired result] but are stuck with [current frustration].
- Turn [messy input] into [valuable output] in [timeframe].
- The [category] for [ICP] who need [urgent job-to-be-done].
- Stop [costly behavior]. Start [better mechanism].

## Offer Positioning

Use this structure:

For [ICP], [offer] helps [outcome] by [mechanism], without [pain/risk].

## CTA Patterns

- Get the teardown.
- Book the fit call.
- See the sprint plan.
- Send me the audit.
- Start with the scorecard.

## Email Sequence Shape

1. Problem recognition.
2. Cost of inaction.
3. New mechanism.
4. Proof or case study.
5. Objection handling.
6. Offer and CTA.
7. Last call or next best step.

## Ad Angle Types

- Pain callout.
- Desired future.
- Contrarian belief.
- Proof-led.
- Mistake list.
- Before/after.
- Founder story.
- Comparison.
